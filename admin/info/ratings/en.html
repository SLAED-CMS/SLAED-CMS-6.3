[justify]The "Ratings" section allows you to manage the participation of module content in ratings:
[li] [b][i]Profile[/i][/b] – the profile (personal information) of each user is rated;
[li] [b][i]Questions and Answers[/i][/b] – entries of the "Questions and Answers" module are rated;
[li] [b][i]File Catalog[/i][/b] – entries (files) of the "File Catalog" module are rated;
[li] [b][i] Forum[/i][/b] – topics of the "Forum" module are rated (replies within topics are not rated);
[li] [b][i]Help[/i][/b] – messages of the "Help" module are rated;
[li] [b][i]Jokes[/i][/b] – entries (jokes) of the "Jokes" module are rated;
[li] [b][i]Site Catalog[/i][/b] – entries (websites) of the "Site Catalog" module are rated;
[li] [b][i]Media Catalog[/i][/b] – entries of the "Media Catalog" module are rated;
[li] [b][i]News[/i][/b] – entries of the "News" module are rated;
[li] [b][i]Articles[/i][/b] – entries of the "Articles" module are rated;
[li] [b][i]Shop[/i][/b] – products of the "Shop" module are rated.

For each of the available modules, the following can be configured:
[li] [b][i]Rating participation interval, in days[/i][/b] – how many days the information about a user's vote will be stored in the system (after the time expires, the user will be able to rate the entry again). This is to prevent rating manipulation by website visitors. Value 0 – no restriction.
[li] [b][i]Activate rating[/i][/b] – whether the corresponding module will have a rating system or not.
[li] [b][i]Allow rating only in detailed view?[/i][/b] – whether the rating scale will be available in the brief view of a module entry or only in the detailed view.

[b][color=green]Rating Calculation Rules[/color][/b]
The rating is a system for tracking user evaluations that they can assign to the corresponding website entries. Ratings are given on a five-point scale. The rating scale can consist of stars or use a plus/minus format.[/justify]
