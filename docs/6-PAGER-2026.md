# Pager 2026

Work plan for the pager of the system — `sl-pager` and `sl-pager-info`: the approved face of the stand page
`public/demo/pg-09-final.html` carried into both themes — and for the segmented switch `sl-seg` with its travelling
knob, the one component the pager, the colour mode of the header and the view of a Node list stand on.

Status: planned, nothing implemented. The order of the batches is kept in `docs/ROADMAP-2026.md`. Update this line
as batches land.

No line numbers anywhere in this document on purpose: every reference names the function, the file or the constant
it points at, and that name is what to search for.

## Decisions

Taken by the owner on 2026-10-08; settled, not to be reopened by a batch. No decision is left open.

- **The face is `public/demo/pg-09-final.html` as it stands on 2026-10-08.** One row: the back arrow, the rail of
  numbers, the forward arrow; to the right of it, behind a hairline, "Наверх" and an "i" button. Under the row one
  quiet line: "Страница 3 из 10 · Показаны 21–30 · Всего 97" — each part after the dot opens with a capital, and
  "Показаны", not "материалы", so the same words fit news, comments, posts, letters and the records of the panel.
- **The rail** is the segmented switch `sl-seg sl-seg-pager` (below): the numbers lie in its sunken track
  (`--sl-bg-muted` with `--sl-shadow-inset`), and the plate under the current page is the brand colour
  (`--sl-primary-strong`, raised) with the number in the text that reads on it (`--sl-on-solid`). The arrows are round
  raised `sl-but-mini` buttons beside the rail; a disabled arrow is transparent and faded.
- **One size everywhere:** every cell, arrow and button is the mini button size `--sl-size-chip`; the digits are
  `--sl-font-small` and bold, the current page too; the line under the rail is `--sl-font-small`. The rail keeps a
  `--sl-space-1` padding, so the current cell sits inside it and never touches its edge, as on `pg-01-rail.html`; the
  buttons stand `--sl-space-2` apart, the hairline keeps `--sl-space-3` on each side.
- **The rail is centred in its column,** the group "Наверх" + "i" stands beside it and does not push it off centre.
- **The line under the rail stays plain text** as on the stand page. The forms tried beside it — board, range, chips,
  bar (`pg-10` … `pg-13`) — are rejected: the row is busy enough.
- **The "i" button explains the arrow keys** in the system tip (`sl-tip sl-float` with `sl-float-panel`) on hover and
  focus: the heading "Листайте с клавиатуры", the keys ← and → drawn as the Bootstrap icons `bi-arrow-left` and
  `bi-arrow-right` in `kbd` caps, labelled for a screen reader, in a list of its own — not a `dl`, which the site tip
  lays out as label and value and the panel tip as "label: value" — with "Предыдущая страница" and "Следующая
  страница", the note "Пока курсор не стоит в поле ввода". The keys page the list the pager belongs to.
- **The pager narrows by its own column,** not by the window: under 520px of its container the far numbers go (the
  first, the last and the neighbours of the current page stay), the range part of the line goes; under 320px the
  neighbours go too, leaving "1 … 8 … 16". The column of the message list of `index.php?name=account&op=privat` —
  329px on a 1440 screen — is the tightest room and must show `‹ 1 … 7 8 9 … 16 › | ↑ i` without overflow.
- **One segmented switch for three places** (owner, 2026-10-08): the colour mode in the site header, the view of a
  Node list and the pager are one component, `sl-seg`, as on the stand (`public/demo/assets/demo.css`). A sunken
  track `sl-seg`, cells `sl-seg-cell`, an empty plate `sl-seg-knob`, `sl-seg-run` for a run of cells a script redraws;
  the chosen cell (`sl-is-active`) recolours its own content over the plate, so no icon is copied into the plate any
  more. A modifier sets only the tokens of its place: `sl-seg-mode` (the gradient of the top band, light icons),
  `sl-seg-view` (a quiet track), `sl-seg-pager` (mini cells, the plate inside the track). Tokens `--sl-seg-height`,
  `--sl-seg-pad-x`, `--sl-seg-pad-y`, `--sl-seg-bg`, `--sl-seg-text`, `--sl-seg-knob-bg`, `--sl-seg-knob-text`,
  `--sl-seg-knob-shadow`. The names `sl-knob` and `--sl-rail-*` are taken by other parts and are not used.
- **The colour of the plate follows the meaning:** a setting (the mode, the view) wears a neutral light plate, a place
  (the current page) the brand colour.
- **The knob travels** (owner, 2026-10-08), in every place and both themes, by one mechanism in `slaed.js`: the rail
  names itself in `data-sl-knob` (`mode`, `view`, `pager`), the plate carries `data-sl-knob-mark`; the script measures
  the chosen cell and writes `--sl-d-knob-x` and `--sl-d-knob-w`, and the sheet moves the plate with `--sl-time-slow`
  and `--sl-ease-in-out`. It follows a change of the chosen cell whoever makes it. A page that is left — a link, the
  POST of the mode form, an htmx swap — hands the place of every rail to the next through `sessionStorage`, so the plate
  travels from where it stood. Without the script the plate stays away and the chosen cell wears it; with
  `prefers-reduced-motion` nothing moves.
- **One "Наверх" for both themes** (owner, 2026-10-08): every "Наверх" — the pager, the lower navigation of
  `search` and `users`, the admin footer — is a plain link to `#top`, and the page scrolls smoothly by
  `scroll-behavior: smooth` on the root of each theme, switched off under `prefers-reduced-motion`. No script and no
  inline handler: `Upper()` of `slaed.js`, the flag `is_upper` and the `OnClick` it prints in the admin `link.html`
  leave. The "Наверх" of the lite footer, hidden for good today, leaves the lite footer.
- **No "i" on a touch screen** (`pointer: coarse`): there are no arrow keys to explain. "Наверх" stays everywhere.
- **Both themes.** The face goes into lite and admin alike; the admin pager leaves its `sl-pnum` row for the same rail,
  steps and tip. **The mechanism and the classes are one:** both themes print the same markup through the same
  fragment structure and the same classes, and only each theme's sheet differs. The admin face is
  `public/demo/pg-14-admin.html` as it stands on 2026-10-08 — the template of `pg-09` byte for byte: in the list foot of
  the panel the row starts at the left edge and the page line "Страница 5 из 337 · Показаны 101–125 · Всего 8414" is
  pushed to the right, next to the bulk action, where the panel prints it today; in a narrow container the line goes
  under the row.
- **The list foot of the panel wraps on a phone.** `.sl-list-bottom` has no narrow rule today, so under 560px the bulk
  action lies over the pager, the old one as well as the new; under 560px the pager takes the whole line and the action
  goes under it.
- **Every pager carries the "i".** Two pagers on one page — a topic with a pager above and below the posts — lead to
  the same pages, so the keys follow the first visible pager and both explain them.
- **The strings.** The line is `_PAGE` with `_NUMOF`, then `_PAGERANGE`, then `_OVERALL` with the total, the parts
  joined by " · " in the fragment; "Всего" loses the colon it has in the line of today ("Всего: 240"). New:
  `_PAGERANGE` "Показаны %1$s–%2$s"; for the tip `_PAGEKEYS` "Листайте с клавиатуры" (also the label of the "i"),
  `_PAGEPREV` "Предыдущая страница", `_PAGENEXT` "Следующая страница", `_PAGEKEYNOTE` "Пока курсор не стоит в поле
  ввода", `_KEYLEFT` "Стрелка влево" and `_KEYRIGHT` "Стрелка вправо" for the icons of the keys; `_PAGENAV`
  "Страницы" for the label of the `nav` (`_PAGES` is "Статьи" and does not fit). The arrows keep `_BACK` and `_NEXT`,
  "Наверх" is `_PAGETOP`. Every name has twelve characters at most and is free today; six locales, the other five
  worded in the batch.

## What exists today

- The colour mode of the site header is `fragments/mode-switch.html` of lite: a POST form with three `sl-mode-cell`
  buttons in `sl-mode-rail` and a `sl-mode-knob` that carries a copy of the chosen icon; `theme.css` steps the knob by
  an `nth-child` ladder for three cells of `--sl-mode-width`. The form reloads the page, so the knob never travels on
  the site today. `--sl-mode-width` and `--sl-mode-height` are also read by the admin `.sl-mode .sl-set-pick`, a part
  that only shares the name.
- The stand carries the whole of the new component and the mechanism: `sl-seg` and the `data-sl-knob` rules in
  `public/demo/assets/demo.css`, `setDemoKnob()` in `public/demo/assets/demo.js`, the mode switch in the stand header,
  `nh-13-calm.html`, and `pg-09` … `pg-14`.
- `getTplPagerView()` in `core/helpers.php` is the one renderer: a window of `$maxpg` numbers round the current page,
  the first and the last outside it, a dots badge where numbers are skipped. It renders every link through the
  fragment `pager-link` and wraps the result in `pager` with `page_label`, `page_of`, `overall` and the caller's meta.
- Callers reach it through `getTplPager()` (counts by a query or takes `count`) and `getPageNumbers()` (known
  counters); the comments render through `getPageNumbers()` in `core/user.php`, the private messages (with an htmx
  target) and `modules/node/index.php` call `getTplPagerView()` directly. The `getPager()` of
  `core/classes/comment.php` and `core/classes/privat.php` only compute the bounds of a page. About thirty call sites in
  `core/`, `modules/` and `admin/modules/`.
- The footer's "Наверх" (`top_link` in `getFootControls()`, `_PAGETOP`) is a link to `#` with `is_top_hidden` and
  `is_upper`. The admin `link.html` turns `is_upper` into `OnClick="Upper('html, body', 600)"` — the only caller of
  `Upper()` in `slaed.js` — and `is_top_hidden` into the visible round `sl-thide` of the panel footer. The lite
  `link.html` reads no `is_upper` and turns `is_top_hidden` into `sl-hidden`, so the lite footer prints
  `<a href="#" title="Наверх" class="sl-hidden">`, which nobody ever sees. The lower navigation of `search` and
  `users` (`navi-lower`) links "Наверх" to `#top`, which the browser takes for the start of the page without an
  element of that id.
- Dead parts found on 2026-10-08, each checked by the whole word over PHP, templates, scripts and tests, no key built
  at run time: `getFootControls()` takes `$debug` that both callers pass empty, so the `debug_html` branch of
  `foot-controls.html` in both themes never fires (the layout prints the debug itself), and both callers pass
  `_PAGETOP` twice as `$title` and `$label`. Keys of `fragments/link.html` no caller passes — lite:
  `is_button_green`, `is_card_category`, `is_card_comment`, `is_card_image`, `is_card_read`, `is_cat_image`,
  `is_cat_name`, `is_comment`, `is_media_ed2k`, `is_media_http`, `is_plus`, `is_read`, `is_related_image`,
  `rel_attr`; admin: `is_button_green`, `is_admin_language_link`, `is_menu_list_image`, `img_class`, `label_span`,
  `label_span_class`, `target_attr`, `toggle_onclick`, `rel_attr`. `is_cat_image` and `is_cat_name` are also read
  by the lite `span.html`, `toggle_onclick` by the admin `toggle-form-block.html`, and passed there by nobody either.
  `setWindowFront()` and `setWindowExpand()` have no caller outside `slaed.js` but are the window API of
  `docs/WINDOW.md` and stay.
- The two theme fragments differ: lite `pager.html` prints `sl-pager-info` as a centred line above the band, admin

- `tools/ui-contract.php` declares `pager`, `pager-dot`, `pager-item` and explains why `.sl-pager`, `.sl-pager-main`

- Arrow keys already belong to the tabs of `setTabs()` in `slaed.js`, to the file manager (`filemanager.js`) and to

## Design

### Segmented switch

`sl-seg` and its modifiers go into the `theme.css` of each theme with the theme's own token values, and are declared
in `tools/ui-contract.php` (`seg`, `seg-knob`). `fragments/mode-switch.html` moves onto `sl-seg sl-seg-mode`; the
`sl-mode-*` rules of lite and the `nth-child` ladder leave. `--sl-mode-bg` stays as the track of `sl-seg-mode`;
`--sl-mode-width` and `--sl-mode-height` leave lite, and the admin keeps its own for `.sl-set-pick`.

### Knob

One function in `slaed.js`, run at start and on `htmx:afterSwap` for the swapped part: it measures the chosen cell of
every `[data-sl-knob]`, sets the plate on the place handed over from the page before without a transition, and lets
it travel on the next frame. The place of every rail is kept on `pagehide` and on `htmx:beforeSwap`, by the name of
the rail; the stand reads it back only on the same path and within five seconds, so a place does not wander into an
unrelated page. A resize observer answers at once when it starts, so it must leave a plate alone while that plate
still stands on the place of the page before — the stand lost the travel through a reload until it did.

### Marks of the window

`getTplPagerView()` hands each number link two marks besides `is_cur`: `is_far` for a number more than one away
from the current page and `is_near` for a neighbour, neither for the first and the last. The `pager-link` of each
theme prints them as classes (`sl-pager-far`, `sl-pager-near`) the theme hides by container query; the selection of
numbers itself does not change. When the meta carries `limit` and `count`, the view also hands `range_from` and
`range_to` to `pager`.

### Fragment

The `pager.html` of each theme becomes a `nav` labelled `_PAGENAV`: the row (`sl-pager-main`) holding the arrows
(`sl-but-mini`) and the rail — `sl-seg sl-seg-pager` with `data-sl-knob="pager"`, its plate `sl-seg-knob`, the numbers
in `sl-pager-pages` as the `sl-seg-run`, each number a `sl-seg-cell` with its mark class — then the side group with
"Наверх" (a link to `#top`) and the tip, then the line `sl-pager-info`. The admin fragment drops its
`sl-pnum` row for the same parts. The current page carries `sl-is-active` and `aria-current="page"`; a disabled arrow
stays a `span`, as today. Class names stay `sl-pager-*` and `sl-seg-*`; new parts are declared in
`tools/ui-contract.php`.

### Theme

`.sl-pager` gets `container-type: inline-size`, the steps are `@container` queries on it. A container of inline size
does not grow by its content, so every place that holds the pager as a flex item must give it a width — the module
foot of the panel does (`.sl-module-foot .sl-pager`), the others are checked; the stand showed a pager collapsing to
nothing in a flex foot without one. Only tokens; the stand's `v9-*` rules for the row, the arrows, the side
group, the line and the tip are the source, renamed into the declared `sl-pager-*` parts in both `theme.css`; the rail
is `sl-seg` from batch 0. The context rules for the forum foot, the message foot, the file manager and the panel list
foot are rechecked against the new band and kept only where they still say something; the panel list foot wraps under
the `sm` step (560px).

### Keys

`slaed.js` gets one keydown listener: ← and → without a modifier follow the back or forward link of the pager that
owns the keys, by a click, so an htmx link swaps as it does for the pointer. The owner is the first visible pager of
the page outside an open window; every pager shows the "i". The listener stands aside when the event is already
handled (`defaultPrevented`), when the target is a field, a `select`, an editable, a tab or anything inside an open
`<dialog>`, and when the file manager or a tab list holds the focus.

## Batches

### Batch 0 — segmented switch and knob

`npm run ui:before` on the site header in both modes. `sl-seg` with its three modifiers in both `theme.css`, the
contract updated, the knob function in `slaed.js`, `mode-switch.html` on `sl-seg-mode`, the `sl-mode-*` rules of lite
retired. Tests: a source test in the manner of `DeskKeysTest` that the function keeps a handed-over place still once,
saves on `pagehide` and `htmx:beforeSwap` and runs again on `htmx:afterSwap`. `npm run ui:after`, `npm run ui:gates`;
the mode switch checked in the browser: one press reloads the page and the plate travels to the new mode.

The same batch clears what the pager and the switch would otherwise inherit: `scroll-behavior: smooth` on the root of
both themes under `prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference`, every "Наверх" on `#top`, `Upper()`, `is_upper` and the
admin `OnClick` removed, the hidden "Наверх" out of the lite footer; `getFootControls()` without `$debug` and with
one text for the link, the `debug_html` branch out of both `foot-controls.html`; the dead keys of both `link.html`,
and of the lite `span.html` and the admin `toggle-form-block.html`, removed. Each removal is checked once more by the
whole word over the tree right before it, as the dead-code rule asks; the admin footer link checked in the browser.

### Batch 1 — the pager

`npm run ui:before` on the news list, a forum topic, the message list, a page with comments and an admin list. The
marks `is_far`, `is_near`, `range_from`, `range_to` from `getTplPagerView()`; `pager.html` and `pager-link.html` of
both themes, the numbers in `sl-seg sl-seg-pager` with `data-sl-knob="pager"`; the band in both `theme.css`, the
context rules of the forum foot, the message foot, the file manager and the panel list foot rechecked, the panel list
foot wrapping under 560px; `_PAGERANGE` in six locales; the contract updated. Tests: a unit test of the view —
the first and the last always present, the dots where numbers are skipped, the marks on the right numbers for the
first, a middle and the last page and none on the first and the last — and the language validation. `npm run
ui:after`, `npm run ui:gates`; in the browser the message column at 329px, and the plate travelling after a page
change of the news list and after the htmx swap of the message list.

### Batch 2 — keys, tip and reference

The tip strings in six locales, the "i" in both fragments, the keys in `slaed.js`. A source test in the manner of
`DeskKeysTest`: the listener stands aside for fields, tabs and open windows and clicks the link rather than navigating
itself. In `docs/TEMPLATES.md` the segmented switch and the knob — the parts, the modifiers, the tokens, the contract
of `data-sl-knob` — and the pager — the fragment, the marks, the steps, the keys; the change in `docs/VERSIONS.md`.
