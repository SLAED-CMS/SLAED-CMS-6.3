# Ranks 2026

Work plan for the rank of a group — the row of marks under an avatar in the forum, the comments, the profile and the
user block: the picture files of the theme give way to Bootstrap Icons, the group list of the panel becomes a ladder,
and the group form gets a rank composer.

Status: planned, nothing implemented. The order of the batches is kept in `docs/ROADMAP-2026.md`. Update this line
as batches land.

No line numbers anywhere in this document on purpose: every reference names the function, the file or the constant
it points at, and that name is what to search for.

## Decisions

Taken by the owner on 2026-10-09; settled, not to be reopened by a batch. No decision is left open.

### The rank

- **Icons, not pictures.** A rank is drawn with Bootstrap Icons, which both themes already carry as a required asset
  (the vendor list of the theme package in `core/system.php`). The folder `public/templates/lite/images/ranks/` and
  every read of it leave.
- **"N of M".** A rank is a row of M marks of one icon, the first N lit in the colour of the rank, the rest muted —
  the face of today's pictures, where `rank_3.png` is three lit stars of five. N = M gives a plain row of N.
  0 ≤ N ≤ M, 1 ≤ M ≤ 10.
- **A muted mark is the same icon in a muted tone** of the theme, not an outline variant: it works for every icon of
  the catalogue, also those without a `-fill` pair.
- **A colour of its own.** The rank carries its own colour beside the colour of the group (`_groups.color`, which
  paints the name and the ring of the avatar). This keeps today's faces: the moderators wear a violet group and gold
  stars.
- **One column, one string.** The rank lives in the existing `_groups.rank` (`VARCHAR(255)`) as
  `icon:lit:total:#rrggbb`, for example `star-fill:3:5:#8297a2`. The schema does not change, and every query that
  reads `rank` today keeps reading it. The column changes meaning from a file name to this string. "Bound to the
  group colour" is not stored: the form shows it bound while the two colours are equal.
- **One contrast floor: 3:1 on both surfaces.** One stored colour serves the light and the dark theme, and no colour
  reaches 4.5:1 on white and on the dark surface at once (it would need a luminance at most 0.18 for the one and at
  least 0.22 for the other). 3:1 is the floor both can meet — WCAG 1.4.11 for the marks, the large-text floor of
  1.4.3 for the bold name — and it binds the migration and both palettes of the form. A colour the administrator
  picks below it is warned about, never refused.
- **The migration writes the darkened tones.** The colours of the pictures fail 3:1 on the light surface; the
  migration writes the same hue darkened to pass both: steel `#a1bbc8` → `#8297a2`, gold `#ffd170` → `#b0904d`, bronze
  `#f4a65a` → `#c68649`, sky `#81cafb` → `#629abf`.

### The panel

The face is `public/demo/grp-10-unified.html` as it stands on 2026-10-09 (the stand, series "Группы и ранги").

- **«Главная» is two lists of one component, the ladder step.** First, full width, «Лестница пунктов» with the subtitle
  «Участник поднимается сам, набирая пункты»: the point groups by threshold. Second, «Специальные группы» with
  «Участников назначает администратор, порог не действует». A step is: the threshold column, a node in the group
  colour on the rail, the rank and then the name in the group colour with the description under them, the share of
  members as a bar under «Участников: N», and the dial of the panel at the end. A special group shows the mark
  «Специальная» with `bi-patch-check-fill` where the threshold stands, and its rail is dashed: no sequence.
- **A painted name stays readable in both themes.** Wherever a group name wears its colour, the theme keeps the hue and
  clamps the lightness: `light-dark(oklch(from <colour> min(l, 0.5) c h), oklch(from <colour> max(l, 0.7) c h))`.
  This holds 4.5:1 for any stored colour (owner, 2026-10-09, after the UI/UX audit); the stored colour is untouched.
  The contrast tiles of the form measure the name as it is painted.
- **The name of a step is a link to its group** in the form (owner, 2026-10-09).
- **Each list has its own sort button,** `bi-sort-numeric-down` / `bi-sort-numeric-up-alt` at the right of its
  heading: the ladder by threshold (ascending first), the special groups by members (most first). It reverses the
  order in place; nothing is stored.
- **«Участников: N» is a link** to the user list of the group, the address the dial of the list carries today as
  «Подробный просмотр» (`admin.php?op=users_show&search=6&chng_user=<id>`). A group without members shows
  «Пусто — участников нет» and only the dashed outline of its bar.
- **No «Новая группа» button on the list.** The tab «Добавить» is the way in.
- **«Добавить» has no head:** the preview of the console already shows the rank, the tab says what the screen is.
  It holds three numbered parts:
  1. «Готовый ранг» — «Щелчок применяет, дальше можно подстроить»: eighteen presets in rows of 9, 6 or 3 by the
     width, so the rows are always full.
  2. «Свой ранг» — the console: forty frequent icons in two even lines of twenty (a grid of twenty columns, flush with the
     picker) and the existing `icon-picker` with
     `getAdminIconWindow()` under it for the whole catalogue; sliders «Всего значков» (1–10) and «Горит» (0 – all);
     «Цвет ранга» as a row of circles. Beside it the preview: the rank at the three sizes it is printed in (14px,
     18px, 24px), one under another without captions and flush right, then two tiles, «Светлая тема» and «Тёмная тема», one above the
     other. A tile holds «Ранг N:1» at the left with its caption at the right, the rank flush right under them, «Имя N:1» at the
     left with the name at the right; a warning line follows when either is below 3:1. Ten marks of 24px must fit: the preview column is
     320px, and it stands as tall as the controls beside it, which keep their own height from the top.
  3. «Группа» — name, description, «Цвет группы», «Спец. группа», «Порог пунктов». The threshold row is hidden for a
     special group.
- **Every colour is picked one way, by circles.** The rank: sixteen swatches in hue order, then «Свой цвет» (a hue
  wheel holding the native colour field, ringed when the value is in no swatch), then, behind a hairline, «Как цвет
  группы» (a circle in the group colour with `bi-link-45deg`; while it is chosen the rank follows the group colour).
  The group: fourteen swatches and «Свой цвет». Each row spreads across the full width of its field, its first and
  last member flush with the fields above and below. Every swatch passes the 3:1 floor.
- **A new group arrives as the next step of the ladder,** filled by the server: threshold half again above the top
  step, rounded up to a thousand (to 25 below a thousand), the icon of the top step with one more lit mark (one more
  mark too when all were lit, at most ten), the rank colour of the top step, the group colour that follows the top
  one in the palette (the first swatch when it is in none). An info alert says «Предложена следующая ступень после
  «X»: порог, ранг и цвет уже заполнены. Осталось назвать группу.»
- **The threshold says where the group will stand,** in words: «Встанет первой ступенью, перед «X» (N пунктов).»,
  «Встанет между «X» (N пунктов) и «Y» (M пунктов).», «Встанет последней ступенью, после «X» (N пунктов).», and for a
  taken threshold «Такой порог уже у «X» (N пунктов): выберите другой.»
- **Leaving a changed form asks,** through the confirm window of the panel (`setConfirmTask()` of `slaed.js`): «Изменения
  группы не сохранены. Уйти без сохранения?». A tab, «Отмена» and the browser's own ways out (`beforeunload`) are
  guarded; the save is not.
- **The rows of choices are toolbars** (`role="toolbar"`): one stop for Tab, the arrows move inside, up and down by a
  whole line of the row, Home and End to the ends; the chosen member holds the stop.
- **Every caption is a sentence and opens with a capital,** and every caption passes 4.5:1: the marks under the
  sliders and «Пусто — участников нет» wear `--sl-text-muted`, never `--sl-text-subtle`.
- **A phone gets the same form.** The forty icons run in twenty, ten or eight columns by the width of the controls (a
  container query), the icon picker lets its button wrap, the controls track is `minmax(0, 1fr)`, a wrapped row of
  circles starts at the left. Below 1100px the mark of a special group keeps its icon and hides the word visually,
  not from a screen reader. The page has no horizontal scroll at 375, 768, 1024 and 1440.
- **A toolbar is set up when its screen is shown,** not only when the form loads: a row prepared while hidden would
  leave a Tab stop on every member.

## What exists today

- **Data.** `_groups.rank` holds a file name of `templates/<theme>/images/ranks/`. The stand has eleven groups:
  six point groups on `rank_0.png` … `rank_5.png` and five special groups on `client.png`, `moderator.png`,
  `partner_bronze.png`, `admin.png`. A fresh install seeds no group (`storage/update/sql/insert.sql`). `_users.rank`
  is the free-text title of a user (`_URANK`), another thing, untouched here.
- **The pictures.** Fifteen files, each 93×17: five marks in a row. Colours sampled from the files: the muted mark
  `#e4edf1`; lit `#a1bbc8` (`rank_1` … `rank_5`, `partner_silver`), `#f4a65a` (`admin`, `partner_bronze`),
  `#ffd170` (`moderator`, `partner_gold`), `#81cafb` (`developer`, `hosting`, `client`).
- **Six readers, each its own way.** All build `getThemeImagePath('ranks/'.$x)` and test it with `file_exists()`:
  - `getCommentView()` in `core/user.php` → `rank_link` of `fragments/comment.html`;
  - the post list of `modules/forum/index.php` → `rank_link` of `fragments/forum-post.html`;
  - the profile of `modules/account/index.php`, through the `rank` of `getUserLevelData()` in `core/user.php` →
    `has_rank_image` / `rank_src` of `partials/account-profile.html`;
  - `blocks/user_info.php` → `rank_url` of `partials/block-user-info.html`;
  - the list of `admin/modules/groups.php` (`groups()`) → a `span` fragment with `img_src` built from
    `$conf['theme']`;
  - the form `add()` of the same file: a `select` over `scandir()` of the folder, an `image-preview` under it, the
    default `rank_1.png`; `save()` strips the folder prefix off the posted path.
- **The list** is a sortable table (`sl-table-list-sort`) of all groups in one order, ranks as pictures, members as a
  number; `groups()` counts the members of each group with a query of its own.
- **Theme.** Lite `theme.css` has `.sl-profile-rank-img`, sized by `--sl-rank-height` of `base.css`. The name `rank`
  is already a component of `tools/ui-contract.php`. The admin `theme.css` fixes every `.bi` to
  `--sl-size-icon-xs`.
- **Help.** `admin/info/groups/*.md` describes the picture select and the folder.

## Design

### The string and the helper

One parser and one renderer in `core/system.php`, beside `getCategoryIcon()`:

- `getRankData(string $rank): array` — splits `icon:lit:total:#rrggbb`; the icon must match `^[a-z0-9-]+$`, the
  colour `^#[0-9a-f]{6}$`, the numbers the bounds above. Anything else — an old file name included — gives an empty
  array, and the rank is not drawn.
- `getRankView(string $rank, string $title): string` — the `rank` fragment of the theme, or an empty string. `$title`
  is the group line every reader already builds (`_GROUP.': '.$name` or `_RANK`); it becomes the `title` and the
  accessible name, the marks themselves are `aria-hidden`.

Every reader of the six passes the raw column and its title; none touches a path any more. The template keys
(`rank_link`, `rank_src`, `rank_url`, `has_rank_image`) become one `rank_html` per template.

### Fragment and theme

- `fragments/rank.html` in both themes: `<span class="sl-rank" role="img" aria-label title style="--sl-rank-color">`
  with M `<i class="bi bi-<icon>">`, the lit ones marked `sl-is-active`. Both themes print the same markup; only the
  sheets differ.
- The lit mark takes `--sl-rank-color`; the muted one `--sl-border-strong` (no raw `#e4edf1`). The marks stand tight
  (`--sl-space-1`), as on the pictures; `--sl-rank-height` and `.sl-profile-rank-img` leave.
- The marks take the size of the place: `.sl-rank .bi { font-size: 1em; }`. The admin `theme.css` fixes every `.bi`
  to `--sl-size-icon-xs`, so without this rule a rank is 16px in the list and in the preview alike; lite has no such
  rule and inherits already, but carries the same line so both sheets read alike.

### The list

`groups()` prints two blocks of one step fragment, a new admin `fragments/group-step.html`, fed by one query that
counts the members of every group with `GROUP BY` in place of a query per group. The ladder takes the point groups
ordered by threshold; the special block the special groups ordered by members, most first. The step carries the
threshold or the mark, the node, `getRankView()` before the name, the description, the members link or the empty
line, the bar (`--v-share`, the share of the largest group) and the dial with «Полное редактирование» and the delete
it has today. The sort buttons reorder the steps in `admin-ui.js`; the step names its sort keys in `data-` attributes.

### The form

- `add()` builds the three parts. The presets are a list in `admin/modules/groups.php` (`getRankPresets()`): the
  eighteen of the stand, each a lang constant for its name and a rank string. The two palettes are lists beside it
  (`getRankTones()`, `getGroupTones()`), each swatch labelled by its colour name constant.
- For a new group `add()` computes the next step on the server (`getGroupNext()`), so the form opens filled without
  a script; the alert names the top step.
- The console, the swatch rows, the preview sizes, the contrast tiles, the threshold hint, the toolbars and the leave
  guard are one handler in `admin-ui.js` on `[data-sl-rank-form]`. The contrast is computed against the real
  background of each tile: the tiles set `color-scheme: light` and `color-scheme: dark`, and the theme tokens are
  `light-dark()`, so each tile resolves its own surface. The leave guard follows the file editor's in the same
  file: a changed form asks through `setConfirmTask()`, a release flag lets the answered navigation go.
- `save()` reads `rank_icon`, `rank_lit`, `rank_total`, `rank_color`, clamps the numbers, checks icon and colour by the
  same rules as `getRankData()`, and writes the joined string; it refuses a threshold another point group has.
- Lang constants in all six languages for every caption of «Главная» and «Добавить» and the eighteen preset names;
  `_IMG` leaves this form only.

### Migration

At the end of `storage/update/sql/table_update6_3.sql`, one `UPDATE` of `_groups` with a `CASE` over the file names,
touching only values that end in an image extension, so a second run changes nothing:

| File | Becomes |
| --- | --- |
| `rank_0.png` … `rank_5.png` | `star-fill:N:5:#8297a2` |
| `admin.png` | `star-fill:5:5:#c68649` |
| `moderator.png` | `star-fill:5:5:#b0904d` |
| `developer.png` | `star-fill:5:5:#629abf` |
| `client.png` | `record-circle-fill:5:5:#629abf` |
| `hosting.png` | `gear-fill:5:5:#629abf` |
| `partner_silver.png` | `gear-fill:5:5:#8297a2` |
| `partner_bronze.png` | `sun-fill:5:5:#c68649` |
| `partner_gold.png` | `sun-fill:5:5:#b0904d` |
| any other picture (a site's own) | `star-fill:0:5:` + the group colour, or `#8297a2` without one |

## Out of scope

- `_users.rank`, the personal title.
- The ring of the avatar.
- The colours the groups already have: they stay as stored; the form warns about them when one is opened.
- A rank per user beyond the group one.
- The tab «Пункты» (`op=points`).
- The Tab stops of the options dial `sl-dial`: its items are reachable while it is closed, three stops a row, in every
  list of the panel. A step of its own in `docs/ROADMAP-2026.md`.
